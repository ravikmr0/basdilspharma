Our Journey

A Legacy of Healthcare Innovation

Founded in 2009, BASDILS PHARMACEUTICALS PVT. LTD emerged from a vision to revolutionize healthcare accessibility and affordability. What started as a modest pharmaceutical manufacturing unit has grown into a trusted name serving millions of patients across 50+ countries.

Our journey has been marked by consistent commitment to quality, continuous innovation, and unwavering dedication to improving patient outcomes. From our first product launch to our current portfolio of 500+ medicines, every milestone reflects our passion for healthcare excellence.

Today, BASDILS PHARMA stands as a symbol of trust, backed by WHO-GMP certified facilities, state-of-the-art research laboratories, and a team of dedicated pharmaceutical professionals.

Key Milestones

  • ✓ 2009: Foundation of BASDILS PHARMACEUTICALS
  • ✓ 2011: Received WHO-GMP Certification
  • ✓ 2014: Expanded distribution to 20+ countries
  • ✓ 2017: Launched advanced R&D laboratory
  • ✓ 2020: Achieved 500+ product portfolio milestone
  • ✓ 2023: Present in 50+ countries globally
