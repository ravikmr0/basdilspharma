Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lead time for product orders?

Typically 7-14 days depending on order quantity and specific product requirements. Bulk orders may require longer delivery times.

Are your products WHO-GMP certified?

Yes, all our manufacturing facilities and products are WHO-GMP certified, ensuring highest quality standards.

Do you provide custom formulations?

Yes, we offer custom formulation services for bulk orders and corporate clients. Contact our R&D team for details.

How can I become a distributor?

We're always looking for reliable distribution partners. Contact our sales team with your company details and we'll discuss opportunities.

What payment options are available?

We accept bank transfers, cheques, NEFT, RTGS, and corporate credit facilities for approved distributors.

Do you provide technical support?

Yes, our technical team provides complete support for product usage, storage, and handling guidelines.
